# W23 Port-transition candidate (offline only)

This unit will add a versioned analytic plan for the existing 19-case W23 geometry matrix. It retains every full-air Numeric Port polygon and the separate core-capture selection. The candidate supports aligned cases and the four existing single-axis `+/-0.5 deg` output tilts; any pose with both tilt axes nonzero is explicitly unsupported. It does not launch COMSOL, create native entities, authenticate `Qabs`, or claim PML compatibility or scientific acceptance.

For a tilted receiver, use the actual transformed output axis `a` and the one lateral axis `l` in its rotation plane. Set `beta=atan2(a_l,a_x)`, `b=(-sin(beta),cos(beta))` in the `(x,l)` plane, and `w=a cross b` in 3-D. Thus a z-axis tilt uses `l=y,w=+z`; a y-axis tilt uses `l=z,w=-y`. Let `H=6 um`, `L=0.5 um`, backing `B=1.55 um`, axial/lateral PML thickness `D=2 um`, and each lateral Port edge `u0=(sign*H-c_l)/cos(beta)`. The edge starts at `(-L,u0+L*tan(beta))`, turns from `phi0=-beta` through `phiM=-1.5 beta` to `phi2=0`, and ends at `(0,u0)`. The two circular arcs use the frozen chord length and signed radii from the accepted design; their exact endpoints and tangents are recomputed by the verifier. For `beta=0`, use the original straight boundary without dividing by beta.

The two equal chord lengths and signed arc radii are explicitly frozen as `ell=L/(2*cos(beta)*cos(beta/4))`, `R1=ell/(2*abs(sin(beta/4)))`, `R2=ell/(2*abs(sin(3*beta/4)))`, `k1=-sign(beta)/R1`, and `k2=sign(beta)/R2`. For a segment starting at `p0` with tangent angle `phi0` and signed curvature `k`, its center is `C=p0+N(phi0)/k`, where `N(phi)=(-sin(phi),cos(phi))`; its endpoint is `C-N(phi1)/k`. The implementation independently recomputes each endpoint and verifies the two chord vectors sum to `(L,-L*tan(beta))` within a scale-aware floating-point tolerance.

The side-PML transition uses the true signed-distance tube of each arc, not a sheared aperture or the old global-plane distance. Before the initial normal ray the boundary is the original global `l=+/-H` plane; after the terminal normal ray it is the straight `u=u0` backing/cap side. Arc and straight charts share their exact endpoint normals and distances. The complete Port polygon is swept along `a` through the unchanged homogeneous backing and axial cap. Existing unaffected transverse sides and the input axial cap keep their original planes. Each face/edge/corner region is emitted from the axial, piecewise-lateral, and `w` distance families. The executable `classify_port_transition_point(s)` API recomputes the frozen candidate, evaluates signed distances, applies the half-open chart domains, and returns the one matched owner; it does not trust a declared owner count. Exact zero distance belongs to the physical region, positive PML distance uses `(0,D]` (with the published floating-point tolerance), and each seam normal ray belongs to the following chart. The finite ROI is the intersection of the input/output axial halfspaces bounded by their `D` offsets, the physical region between the exact side-centerline graphs and their normal tubes through `D`, and the `w` slab `|w|<=H+D`. It is not clipped by the old global six-face lateral envelope after the curved transition starts, so the true-axis output sweep is preserved. The certificate includes the two lateral tubes for `-L<=s<=0`, both `w` directions through `H+D`, the output backing `0<=s<=B`, and output axial cap `B<=s<=B+D`. It does not claim a global COMSOL geometry partition or native domain IDs.

The certificate is analytic: verify source recipe and case-matrix hashes; recompute Port polygon vertices/area from the existing v2 halfspace clipper; derive arc centers/endpoints, tangent closure and `R>D`; use exact endpoint/critical-angle extrema for transition and offset coordinates; verify the 0.5 um cladding clearance and all local transition/backing/cap x-coordinates outside the `x=+19 um` control-volume face. Prove each tube's Jacobian factor `cos(phi)*(1-sigma*k*d)>0`, its graph-offset separation bound, opposite-side tube separation, endpoint-ray ownership, and the piecewise seam maps. For `phi_max=1.5*abs(beta)`, the certificate derives `m=tan(phi_max)` and `g_min=cos(phi_max)-m*sin(phi_max)>0`; the monotone normal-chart Jacobian and `sigma*(u_offset-f(s_offset))>=d*g_min` give a one-to-one outward strip for `0<=d<=D`. Since the two side graphs are translated by `12/cos(beta)`, their outward strips remain separated. For COMSOL's existing linear polynomial stretch, use `d_tilde=0.775*(1-i)*d`, `delta=d_tilde-d`, and the curved-tube Jacobian factors `0.775*(1-i)` and `(1-sigma*k*d_tilde)/(1-sigma*k*d)`; derive a strict lower bound `abs(1-sigma*k*d_tilde)>=1-0.775*sqrt(2)*D/R_min` and divide by the conservative real denominator upper bound `1+D/R_min`. The verifier recomputes these values from frozen recipe/matrix inputs, rather than accepting caller-supplied PASS values or a recomputed self-hash alone.

The manual basis is COMSOL 6.4 `COMSOL_ReferenceManual.pdf`, p. 482, doc 3627/chunk 12568, SHA-256 `3dacc33243911a98cbcac19c6b83c9b662dd0b7c56b880b3a9e6a2ab602e7106`: the displacement direction is the gradient of its distance function. This is why an independent dual-vector shear is not an admissible substitute. The existing v2 plane-only recipe and its conflict evidence remain immutable; the new schema supersedes that output-side distance profile for these cases only. It must not be accepted by the existing v2 native distance-only adapter. Native geometry IDs, material continuity, PML implementation compatibility, field solution, optical capture, and balance remain `UNVERIFIED`/`NOT_RUN`.
